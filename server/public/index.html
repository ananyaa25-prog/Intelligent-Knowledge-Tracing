Live Knowledge Tracing Assessment

An adaptive assessment that selects the next topic from the model's predicted probability of success, and adjusts each topic's difficulty to your answers.

Context

Foundational maths practice (percents, fractions, linear systems, geometry) in classes where one teacher supports many learners.

Who benefits

Students get questions pitched near their level; teachers see which topic and level each student is stuck on.

Problem

A fixed quiz gives one overall score and hides which topic, and which difficulty level, is the actual gap.

Action

Before each question the model predicts your chance of success; that prediction picks the next topic and difficulty and flags topics that need support.
