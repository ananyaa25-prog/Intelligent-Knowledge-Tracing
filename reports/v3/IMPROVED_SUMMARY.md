# Improved model (GatedKT+) results summary

All numbers are copied from reports/v3/improved_assist.md, improved_eedi_primary.md, improved_eedi_secondary.md
and improved_statics.md (generated by scripts/benchmark_improved.py). Pre-registration: reports/v3/IMPROVED_PREREG.md
(commit c2054fa, written before any test result). Same budget for every deep model: up to 15 epochs, early stopping
(patience 3) on validation AUC. GatedKT+ settings (dropout 0.3, weight decay 1e-5) chosen on the ASSISTments
validation split only (reports/v3/gkt_plus_tuning.md).

gkt_plus_elo was also "better" than dkt_gru, full_gated, logreg_counts and gkt_plus on every dataset (see the
per-dataset reports). On Statics2011 the pretrained gkt_plus_pt was better than gkt_plus (+0.0094) and elo (+0.0179).

## What the gain comes from

- Per-question difficulty (gkt_plus vs full_gated): +0.0060 ASSISTments, +0.0109 Eedi primary, +0.0096 Eedi
  secondary, +0.0169 Statics. This is the main effect.
- The Elo blend adds little AUC (+0.0006 to +0.0050) but improves calibration (ECE 0.0102 vs 0.0221 on ASSISTments).

## Caveats

- Eedi uses 5 seeds: the Wilcoxon test cannot go below p = 0.0625 with 5 pairs; the verdicts rest on the paired
  t-test (Holm) plus both CIs, as pre-registered.
- Statics2011 has only 34 test students per split; bootstrap CIs are correspondingly wide.
- dkt_classic is much weaker than dkt_gru on Eedi and Statics; dkt_gru is the stronger DKT comparator, and
  gkt_plus_elo is also "better" than it everywhere.
- Per-question difficulty only helps for questions seen in training; unseen questions fall back to the skill.
- No time-since-last-attempt feature (not done, see the pre-registration).
- The app's served model is unchanged; GatedKT+ is evaluated offline only.
