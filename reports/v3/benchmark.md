# Benchmark: our model against four baselines (ASSISTments 2009, official split)

Generated by `python scripts/benchmark_baselines_v3.py --seeds 10 --classical-seeds 50 --epochs 10 --max_students 0 --bootstrap 1000` (this run: 8.3 min of new work). Per-seed rows: reports/v3/benchmark_runs.csv. Test students: 313.

## Setup

- Same train / val / test split by student (data/processed/windowed_data.pkl), same targets (every position t >= 1 of a test student, predicted from the history before t), same metrics.
- PFA and BKT: 50 seeds (0-49). BKT seeds change the EM starting point; PFA has no random component (a seed std of 0 means identical results).
- DKT (GRU) and SAKT-lite: 10 seeds (0-9); inputs skill, correct, difficulty bucket; Adam 1e-3, batch 32, 10 epochs, best-validation weights.
- graph_no_gate and full_gated: the existing checkpoints (seeds 42, 123, 2024), wrapped read-only, not retrained. They were trained earlier with their own budget and also read response time / attempts / hints, so they are not under exactly the same budget or inputs (docs/DECISIONS.md D41).
- Max training students: all.

## Interpretation

1. Highest mean test AUC: full gated (ours) (0.8713); lowest: PFA (0.7657).
2. The full gated model improves over: graph, no gate (ours), DKT (GRU), SAKT-lite, PFA, BKT; comparable to: none; baselines that beat it: none.
3. Against the strongest trained deep baseline (DKT (GRU)) the AUC difference is +0.0130 (bootstrap CI [+0.0107, +0.0155]).
4. Full gated: seed 95% CI width 0.0039 (3 checkpoints) vs student-bootstrap width 0.0487; the bootstrap interval is wider, so the choice of test students is the larger source of uncertainty here.
5. Caveat: our checkpoints were trained earlier with a longer budget and extra inputs (response time, attempts, hints), while the deep baselines got 10 epochs on skill, correct and difficulty only; the comparison is therefore not perfectly budget-matched.
