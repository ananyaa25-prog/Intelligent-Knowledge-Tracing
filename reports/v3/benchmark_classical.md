# Classical baselines: multi-seed benchmark (Stage 4)

Generated by `python scripts/benchmark_seeds.py classical --seeds 100 --workers 5`.
Seeds: 0..99 (100 seeds). Run time 8.1 min.
Data: ASSISTments 2009 skill-builder as preprocessed in data/processed/windowed_data.pkl (3119 students, sequences truncated at 500). Targets: every position t >= 1 of each test student predicted from that student's history before t (same targets as the main model).

Metrics: AUC; ACC at threshold 0.5; Brier score (lower is better); ECE with 10 equal-width bins (lower is better). 95% CI = t-interval of the mean over seeds; std uses ddof=1.
